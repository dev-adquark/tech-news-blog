## Traditional Software Development: A Double-Edged Sword

Enter artificial intelligence (AI). AI-driven tools are revolutionizing how we think about software development. Low-code and no-code platforms represent the next big leap in software development. These tools aim to make application development accessible to everyone, regardless of their technical expertise. They provide a visual interface where users can drag and drop components to create applications without writing lines of code.

### Why Malleable Software Matters

Malleable software, enabled by low-code and no-code platforms, combines the best of both worlds.
