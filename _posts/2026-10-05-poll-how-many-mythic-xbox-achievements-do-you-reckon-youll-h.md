The Xbox "Mythic" achievement is a significant milestone for gamers, representing a full completion of an Xbox game. With the recent poll on purexbox.com asking how many 'Mythic' achievements one might achieve, it's a great time to reflect on the gaming journey and the challenges of completing these achievements.

### What Are 'Mythic' Achievements?

'Mythic' achievements are a subset of Xbox achievements that require a full completion of a game, often including all side quests, secret areas, and other hidden content. These achievements are particularly challenging and rewarding, making them a significant goal for many gamers.

### Backdating Full Completions

One interesting aspect of this poll is the backdating of full completions to the Xbox 360 era. This means that if you completed a game on an Xbox 360 and achieved a full completion at the time, it now counts as a 'Mythic' achievement. This can significantly boost your total count, especially for those who have been playing since the early days of Xbox.

### The Current State of Achievement Completion

As of now, the number of 'Mythic' achievements varies widely depending on the player's gaming history. Some gamers might have completed just a handful of games, while others could have over a dozen. The poll aims to gauge the average number of 'Mythic' achievements among the gaming community.

### Factors Influencing Achievement Completion

Several factors can influence how many 'Mythic' achievements a gamer might have:

1. **Game Release Date**: Games released earlier in the console lifecycle had fewer achievements compared to modern titles.
2. **Game Length and Complexity**: Games with more content and side quests tend to have more 'Mythic' achievements.
3. **Gamer Dedication**: Some gamers are more dedicated to achieving full completions than others.
4. **Console Generation**: The Xbox 360 had fewer games overall compared to later generations, which could affect the number of 'Mythic' achievements.

### Notable Games and Their Achievements

Some games are known for having particularly challenging 'Mythic' achievements:

- **The Witcher 3**: While not an Xbox exclusive, The Witcher 3 is known for its complex narrative and numerous hidden achievements.
- **RuneScape 4**: Although announced for a future release, the game is being built in Unreal Engine, suggesting a rich and complex world that could offer numerous 'Mythic' achievements.
