Nintendo and Velan Studios have rolled out the latest update for Star Fox, version 1.2.0, which introduces a variety of exciting new features and content to the classic space shooter. This update adds new stages to Battle Mode, enhancing the game’s replay value and providing players with fresh challenges.

Battle Mode is a key component of Star Fox, offering players a range of missions and objectives to complete. The addition of new stages will undoubtedly keep fans engaged for longer periods. Moreover, the update now supports split-screen play for up to four players on a single Switch console. This feature is particularly welcome for those who enjoy multiplayer gaming and want to share the experience with friends or family members.

The new stages in Battle Mode are designed to challenge players at different skill levels. Whether you're a seasoned veteran or just starting out, these new levels should provide a good balance of difficulty and fun. Additionally, the split-screen support for four players means that everyone can join in the action without needing multiple controllers or devices.

For those who are curious about the performance of Star Fox on the Nintendo Switch, it's worth noting that the game runs smoothly. The update doesn't introduce any significant performance issues, ensuring a consistent and enjoyable gaming experience.
