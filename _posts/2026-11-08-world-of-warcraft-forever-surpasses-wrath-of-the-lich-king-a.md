World of Warcraft has always been a titan in the gaming industry, but its latest re-release, *World of Warcraft: Forever*, has shattered all previous records. According to Blizzard Entertainment, *Forever* is the biggest launch in WoW history, surpassing even the highly successful *Wrath of the Lich King* expansion. This milestone underscores the enduring appeal and massive fan base of the game.

### The Impact of World of Warcraft: Forever

The sheer scale of *World of Warcraft: Forever*'s launch can be attributed to several factors. Firstly, the game's accessibility has never been higher. Players no longer need to purchase expensive expansions or subscribe to the full version; instead, they can jump right into the game with a one-time purchase. This streamlined approach has made it easier for both new and returning players to dive into the vast world of Azeroth.

Blizzard has reported that the number of level 60 characters created within three days of the launch is unprecedented. This rapid growth indicates a significant influx of new players who are eager to experience classic content alongside longtime fans. The overwhelming demand poses a challenge for Blizzard's retail arm, as they must quickly scale up to meet the increased sales volume.

### Preserving Gaming Legacy

While *World of Warcraft: Forever* steals the spotlight, it's worth noting that the gaming industry as a whole is making strides in preserving classic titles. In a related development, *Ars Technica* reports that EA has been involved in re-releasing classic games like *Wing Commander* and *Ultima* titles on Steam and GOG. Although these releases have not garnered the same level of hype as *World of Warcraft: Forever*, they represent an important effort to keep beloved classics accessible to new generations of gamers.

EA's preservation efforts, while not as explosive as *World of Warcraft: Forever*'s launch, highlight the broader trend of maintaining the legacy of classic games. These re-releases ensure that timeless titles continue to find new audiences and maintain their cultural significance.

### Conclusion

In conclusion, *World of Warcraft: Forever* has set a new benchmark for game launches, eclipsing even the highly regarded *Wrath of the Lich King*. Its success underscores the enduring appeal of classic MMOs and the importance of making such titles accessible to modern audiences. As the gaming landscape continues to evolve, initiatives like those by EA to preserve classic games serve as a reminder of the value of maintaining our gaming heritage.
