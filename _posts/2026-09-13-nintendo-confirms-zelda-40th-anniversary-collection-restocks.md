title: "Nintendo Confirms Zelda 40th Anniversary Collection Restocks"
date: 2026-09-13

Nintendo has announced that the Zelda 40th Anniversary Collection will be restocked via the Nintendo Store app. This news comes as a pleasant surprise to fans of the legendary series, who have been eagerly awaiting the availability of these special editions.

The Zelda 40th Anniversary Collection is a celebration of the franchise's rich history, featuring remastered versions of classic games from the series. The collection originally launched with limited availability, but now it seems that fans can look forward to another chance to secure their favorite titles.

### What to Expect

While the exact details of the restock are still being finalized, it’s likely that the collection will include all the classic games that were originally part of the collection. These typically include titles like "The Legend of Zelda," "A Link to the Past," and "Ocarina of Time," among others. Fans can expect to see high-quality remasters that offer enhanced graphics and sound, ensuring that the experience remains fresh for both long-time fans and newcomers alike.

### How to Get In

Nintendo plans to announce the restock through its official Nintendo Store app. This means that players need to have the app installed on their devices to stay updated on the latest news. The app serves as a one-stop-shop for all things Nintendo, providing notifications about new releases, discounts, and restocks.

### The Importance of the Restock

For many fans, this restock is not just about acquiring more copies of beloved games. It’s also a significant milestone in the franchise's journey. The Legend of Zelda series has been a cornerstone of Nintendo's success for decades, and the 40th anniversary collection serves as a testament to its enduring popularity and influence.

### Community Reaction

The news has already sparked excitement within the gaming community. Many fans are thrilled at the prospect of owning the collection once again and sharing it with friends. Social media platforms have seen a surge in discussions about the collection, with users sharing their thoughts and reminiscing about their favorite Zelda moments.

### Conclusion

While the specifics of the restock are yet to be fully revealed, it’s clear that Nintendo is committed to honoring the legacy of The Legend of Zelda series. Whether you’re a seasoned player or a newcomer, there’s no denying the appeal of these remastered classics. Stay tuned to the Nintendo Store app for updates and prepare to dive into the magical world of Hyrule once more.

This restock could mark the beginning of an exciting era for Zelda enthusiasts, reminding us why the series continues to captivate players around the world.
