In today's digital age, it's easy to come across stories that highlight the worst in human behavior. One such list from BuzzFeed, titled "23 Awful, Selfish, Entitled People Who Felt The World Owed Them Everything," serves as a stark reminder of how entitled some individuals can be. While these stories may seem extreme and isolated, they reflect a broader issue that resonates with many.

The list, which BuzzFeed published, showcases individuals who have made it abundantly clear that they feel they deserve special treatment or outcomes without putting in the necessary effort. These cases range from celebrities to everyday people, all sharing a common thread of entitlement and self-importance. From demanding unrealistic timelines to expecting preferential treatment, these individuals often find themselves at odds with reality and the expectations of those around them.

While such stories are often amusing or even entertaining, they also serve as a wake-up call. They remind us that despite our best efforts to create inclusive and fair societies, there will always be those who feel they should have more than their fair share. This sense of entitlement can manifest in various ways, from demanding free services to expecting others to bend rules for their convenience.

### Entitlement in the Digital Age

Social media platforms have become breeding grounds for this kind of behavior. People can now voice their grievances and demands publicly, often without considering the consequences or the impact on others. The ease of sharing and the echo chambers created by social networks can amplify these voices, making it harder for others to ignore or address the underlying issues.

One notable example comes from the gaming industry. In an interview with Push Square, NIS America's boss, Ken Pohlmann, discussed the feedback from customers regarding the PlayStation 5 disc format. He noted, "Sony's Hearing a Lot of the Feedback from Customers." This feedback, while valid, highlights how consumers sometimes feel entitled to certain features or changes without understanding the complexities involved.

### Fast Cars and Electric Vehicles

While the issue of entitlement might seem far removed from everyday life, it's worth noting that even in the world of high-performance vehicles, there is a similar phenomenon. Electric vehicles (EVs) have been making significant strides in recent years, and some models are capable of impressive acceleration. For instance, Jalopnik highlighted a list of EVs that can hit 60 mph in under 3.5 seconds. Models like the Hyundai Ioniq 5 N, Tesla Model 3 Performance, and Porsche Macan Turbo Electric demonstrate the rapid advancements in electric vehicle technology.

These high-performance EVs showcase the incredible capabilities of modern automotive engineering, but they also bring to light another aspect of consumer behavior. As these vehicles become more accessible, there's a growing expectation among buyers that they should perform as well as—or better than—their internal combustion engine counterparts. This trend reflects a broader cultural shift towards sustainability and innovation, yet it also underscores the ongoing struggle between consumer expectations and practical realities.

### Conclusion

In conclusion, while stories of entitled individuals can be humorous or shocking, they serve as important reminders about the need for balance and responsibility in our society. Whether it's in the gaming industry or the automotive world, the concept of entitlement can lead to misunderstandings and conflicts. It's crucial for individuals to recognize their privileges and responsibilities, ensuring that they contribute positively to the communities around them.

As we move forward, it's essential to foster environments where everyone feels valued and respected, while also encouraging a sense of accountability and fairness. By doing so, we can build a more equitable and harmonious world, one where entitlement is not seen as a right but rather as a choice that should be exercised responsibly.
