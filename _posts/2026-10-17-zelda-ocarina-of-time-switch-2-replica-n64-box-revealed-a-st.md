Nintendo has recently unveiled a special edition of its upcoming Zelda: Ocarina of Time Switch 2, which comes with a replica N64 box. This limited-time bonus is available exclusively in France, offering fans a nostalgic nod to the game's original release.

## The Replica N64 Box

The new Zelda: Ocarina of Time Switch 2 "Replica N64 Box" is a physical reminder of the game's roots, featuring a design reminiscent of the iconic Nintendo 64 console. This special edition not only captures the essence of the original game but also serves as a collector's item for those who cherish the history of the franchise.

### Availability and Pricing

While the exact price and availability details have not been disclosed, it's clear that this bonus is reserved for French consumers. The replica box adds an extra layer of excitement for Zelda fans who are eagerly awaiting the Switch 2 release. For many, owning a piece of gaming history in a tangible form is a significant draw.

### Collector's Edition Appeal

For collectors and long-time fans of the series, the replica N64 box elevates the game beyond just another digital download. It represents a tangible connection to the past and a way to showcase one's passion for the franchise. This limited-edition bonus could potentially drive more interest in the game among casual players who might be drawn to the nostalgic appeal.

## Digital Game Ownership Rights

Amidst these hardware revelations, there's also a push for more control over digital game ownership. Democratic Congressman Ritchie Torres is introducing legislation aimed at ensuring that consumers own their digital games rather than merely holding licenses. This move reflects a growing concern among gamers about the rights and freedoms associated with the digital content they purchase.

### The Right to Own

The proposed bill seeks to give players total ownership over their digital games, similar to how physical media works today. This could mean that users would retain their game data even if they stop paying for a subscription service. Such legislation would provide a level of security and permanence that many gamers desire.

### Impact on Gaming Industry

If passed, this legislation could have significant implications for the gaming industry. Developers and publishers would need to adapt their business models to accommodate a shift in consumer expectations regarding digital ownership. It could lead to changes in pricing strategies, subscription models, and overall customer experience.
