The recent announcement of Final Fantasy 7 Revelation has garnered significant attention, particularly due to its substantial file size. In an interview with Video Games Chronicle, the game's director, Hiroshi Hamaguchi, addressed concerns regarding the game's 200GB size, as well as plans for additional downloadable content (DLC) and a new detail regarding Tifa Lockhart.

## FF7 Revelation: Size Matters, But Not in the Way You Think

One of the most pressing questions surrounding FF7 Revelation has been its enormous file size. At 200GB, the game is significantly larger than many other titles currently on the market. However, Hamaguchi reassures fans that this large footprint is necessary to deliver a high-quality experience. "We wanted to ensure that FF7 Revelation would be a seamless, immersive adventure," he explained. "This includes detailed graphics, expansive environments, and a richly textured story."

The game's size also reflects the inclusion of extensive cutscenes and additional content that enhances the player's experience. Hamaguchi emphasized that while the file size may seem daunting, it's a testament to the team's commitment to quality over quantity. "Our goal was to create a game that players could lose themselves in, and we believe that the extra storage space is worth it."

## DLC Plans and Future Content

Hamaguchi also touched on the game's DLC plans, which are expected to provide additional value to players. While specifics were not disclosed, he hinted at new areas, characters, and missions that will expand upon the main storyline. "We're working hard to bring more of Cloud, Tifa, and Co. to life," he said. "There's a lot more to discover in the world of FF7."

The director's comments suggest that FF7 Revelation will offer a robust post-launch experience, encouraging players to return long after completing the main campaign. This approach aligns with the trend of providing ongoing content to keep players engaged, a strategy that has proven successful in other popular franchises.

## A New Tifa Detail Revealed

In addition to discussing the game's size and future content, Hamaguchi revealed a new detail about Tifa Lockhart. While the specifics were not fully elaborated, it's clear that there are exciting developments in store for her character. "Tifa has always been a fan favorite," Hamaguchi noted. "We're excited to bring new depth and complexity to her story."

This revelation adds another layer of anticipation for FF7 Revelation enthusiasts, who have been eagerly awaiting new information about their beloved characters.
